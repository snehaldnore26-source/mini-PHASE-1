Gajar Ka Halwa

Gajar Ka Halwa is a traditional Indian sweet made using fresh carrots, milk, sugar, and ghee. It is especially popular during winter season.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg carrots (grated)
  • 1 liter full cream milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 4 tbsp ghee
  • Dry fruits (cashews, almonds)
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder

Instructions

  1. Wash, peel, and grate the carrots.
  2. Heat milk in a pan and add grated carrots.
  3. Cook on medium flame until milk thickens.
  4. Add sugar and mix well.
  5. Add ghee and cook until halwa leaves the pan.
  6. Add cardamom powder and dry fruits.
  7. Serve hot or warm.
